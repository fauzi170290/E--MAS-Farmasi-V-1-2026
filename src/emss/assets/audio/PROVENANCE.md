# E-MAS Farmasi bundled alert audio

The six WAV files in this directory are original mathematical tone sequences.
They are generated entirely by `tools/generate_original_audio.py` with Python's
standard library. They contain no voice, music, samples, external recordings,
online service output, account identifiers, or credentials. Copyright in this
original asset set is held with the E-MAS Farmasi application and is licensed
for distribution with the application.

The sounds are operational cues only. They do not replace review of the visible
screening result or verification that the workstation speaker is audible.
